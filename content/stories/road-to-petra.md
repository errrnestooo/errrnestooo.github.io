The road to Petra did not announce itself. It did not rise like a curtain or arrive with music. It simply continued, pale and patient, through a country of stone.

I had expected the desert to feel empty. Instead, it felt occupied by time.

Before Petra, there is a strange hour when you are close enough to believe in arrival but still far enough to belong to the road.
